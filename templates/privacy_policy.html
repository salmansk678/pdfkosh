Zero Storage • In-Memory Architecture

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 • Effective immediately

1. The Core Guarantee: 100% In-Memory Processing

At PDFKosh, your privacy is our foundational architecture, not an afterthought. When you upload any document (PDF, JPG, PNG, etc.):

  • Zero Disk Storage: Your files are held strictly in temporary RAM memory streams (io.BytesIO) during the active processing operation.
  • No Permanent Copies: Nothing is ever written to our server's hard disk drives, databases, or cloud buckets.
  • Instant Destruction: As soon as your download begins or the HTTP request finishes, memory is cleared and all references are discarded immediately.
  • Zero Content Scanning: We do not inspect, read, OCR, train artificial intelligence models on, or share your document contents with any third party.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because PDFKosh operates without user accounts or registrations:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • We do not store the documents or images you upload.
  • We do not store passwords entered in the Protect or Unlock PDF tools.

3. Technical Log Data

Like standard web services, our hosting infrastructure may temporarily record generic web server logs, which include your IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and the timestamp of requests. These logs are used purely for security diagnostics, DDoS mitigation, and server performance monitoring, and are never linked to personal identities.

4. Cookies, Web Beacons & Third-Party Advertising

To keep PDFKosh 100% free for everyone around the world, we may serve advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized or contextual ads to you based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.

Note: Advertising cookies never have access to your uploaded PDF files or documents.

5. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under European GDPR and California CCPA privacy frameworks, users have rights regarding their data. Because PDFKosh does not retain personal information or files, there is no personal data stored on our servers to modify, delete, or sell. We do not sell your personal data.

6. Children's Information

PDFKosh does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete it.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out through our official GitHub repository at github.com/salmansk678/pdfkosh.
