Our Mission & Vision

Free, Private & Secure
PDF Tools for Everyone

We believe that basic document utilities should be fast, unlimited, and respect your privacy — without mandatory signups, paywalls, or covert cloud storage.

Why We Built PDFKosh

Every day, millions of students, lawyers, doctors, accountants, and creators need to perform simple tasks like combining two PDFs, splitting pages, or compressing an invoice. Unfortunately, the web is flooded with services that:

❌ Invasive Paywalls

Charging hefty monthly subscriptions just to split or rotate a couple of pages.

❌ Email Harvesting

Forcing mandatory registration and spam newsletters before giving your download.

❌ Privacy Hazards

Storing sensitive personal and financial documents on persistent third-party hard drives.

PDFKosh was engineered to be the antidote: a clean, blazingly fast suite of 9 powerful tools running 100% in volatile memory streams, leaving zero footprint on our servers.

How It Works Under the Hood

Our backend leverages cutting-edge open-source Python engineering:

  • Volatile RAM Buffers: All file streams are ingested into Python's io.BytesIO buffers directly in volatile system memory.
  • Battle-Tested Engines: We utilize trusted libraries like PyPDF, Google's PDFium, ReportLab, and Pillow for pixel-perfect fidelity.
  • Immediate Cleanup: Once the output stream is delivered to your browser, the temporary memory buffer is freed instantly. Nothing ever touches disk storage.
